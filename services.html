  • Color palette and type systems
  • Mood boards and direction sets
  • Brand voice and messaging

02 / Web

Website design & development

Hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on edge hosting. No WordPress, no page builder, no plugin that breaks your site at 3am because it auto-updated.

  • Analytics and conversion tracking

Platform engineering

The site is the front door. We build the rest of the house — the forms that route, the payments that clear, and the automations that follow up while you sleep.

  • Serverless workers and API endpoints
  • Stripe checkout and payment links
  • CRM pipelines and email automation
  • Gated downloads and member areas
  • Progressive web apps and point of sale

04 / Print

Book design & production

Full book development, from raw manuscript to a listing customers can buy. We speak printer, not just browser — spine math, bleed, gutter, and all.

  • Interior typesetting and layout
  • Paperback, hardcover, and ebook builds
  • Cover design and wrap-around files
  • ISBN, metadata, and category strategy
  • Retail listing setup and author pages
  • Launch graphics and marketing assets
05 / Global

Translation & world editions

Your book, published properly in another language — not run through a machine and shipped. Localized covers, correct typography, and edition-specific artwork.

  • Full-manuscript translation
  • Localized cover and spine artwork
  • Language-correct typography and hyphenation
  • Back-translation quality checks
  • Per-market metadata and keywords
  • Editions currently shipping in 4 languages
06 / Audio

Audiobook production

Narrated audiobooks produced in-house, including cloned-voice narration so the author reads their own book without spending three weeks in a booth.

  • Voice cloning and narration
  • Chapter splitting and mastering
  • Retail-spec audio delivery
  • Audio cover art
  • Hosted, gated audio libraries
  • Podcast and episode production
