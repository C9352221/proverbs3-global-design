Sermon-to-Book.

You've preached the message a hundred times. Let's turn it into a book — without you writing a word.

Most ministers have years of preached content sitting on YouTube doing nothing. They want to publish, but they don't have time to write a book from scratch. We have already done this ourselves — turning four conference messages into a 14-chapter published book. We can do the same with yours.

  1. You send 4-10 YouTube links to your sermons on a single theme
  2. We pull the transcripts and organize the content into chapters
  3. We edit the spoken style into clean, readable prose
  4. We add scripture references, callouts, and study questions
  5. You receive a polished manuscript ready for KDP — no writing required
